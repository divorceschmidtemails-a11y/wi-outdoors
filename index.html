How the score works

These are rules of thumb, not a guarantee:
  • Water temp: no USGS gauge on this stretch of the Mississippi reports water temperature (closest Mississippi gauges at Brownsville and McGregor report stage/flow only). Enter a thermometer or sonar reading below. Fall cooling from about 60°F down to 45°F tends to be a strong feeding period. Under 40°F, fish slow down and stack in the tailwaters.
  • River trend: stable or slowly falling water is generally best. Fish wing dams and current breaks. Rising or muddy water pushes fish to slack areas: backwater/slough mouths, behind islands, and close to the bank.
  • Wind: 5–15 mph "walleye chop" helps, especially onto wind-blown points and wing dams. Over 20 mph is unsafe, so fish sheltered water.
  • Pressure: falling before a front tends to be good. A sharp rise after a front ("bluebird") is tough, so slow down and go deeper.
  • Light: dawn, dusk and cloudy days are better.
  • Season: late fall into winter, walleye and sauger concentrate in the tailwater below Lock & Dam 8 (Genoa). Always follow posted restricted areas near the dam.

Mississippi Boundary Waters (WI–MN / WI–IA)

The Mississippi has its own boundary-water seasons and bag limits (with reciprocal license rules for MN/IA) that differ from inland waters. Specific limits: check DNR — "Boundary Waters" table in the 2026–2027 Hook & Line Guide. Victory is on Pool 9 (WI–IA border just south of the MN line).
