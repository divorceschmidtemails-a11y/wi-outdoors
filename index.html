Mississippi Boundary Waters (WI–MN / WI–IA)

The Mississippi has its own boundary-water seasons and bag limits (with reciprocal license rules for MN/IA) that differ from inland waters. Specific limits: check DNR — "Boundary Waters" table in the 2026–2027 Hook & Line Guide. Victory is on Pool 9 (WI–IA border just south of the MN line).

Landings & Refuge

Victory and Genoa landings: locations/status not verified — check DNR / refuge map. Find landings: DNR Public Access Lands map.

Much of the river around Victory is in the Upper Mississippi River National Wildlife & Fish Refuge. It has closed areas during waterfowl season and its own rules on stands, blinds, and boats. Specifics: check refuge (refuge hunting/fishing regulations brochure at that link).
